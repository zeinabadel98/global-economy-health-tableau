# Global Economy, Health & Connectivity | Tableau

## Project Overview

This project explores the relationship between economic development, healthcare outcomes, and internet connectivity across different countries.

I used Tableau to analyze global indicators, compare countries, and identify patterns that could help explain differences in quality of life.

## Tools Used

- Tableau Desktop
- Tableau Public
- Data Visualization
- Dashboard Design
- Exploratory Data Analysis

## Business Questions

- How does GDP per capita relate to life expectancy?
- Is higher internet penetration associated with stronger economic performance?
- What is the relationship between birth rates and infant mortality?
- Which countries perform better or worse across economic and health indicators?
- What patterns can be identified when comparing countries?

## Visualizations

The project includes interactive dashboards and a Tableau Story covering:

- Wealth vs Life Expectancy
- GDP per Capita vs Internet Penetration
- Birth Rate vs Infant Mortality
- Life Expectancy Distribution
- Economic & Health Heatmap
- World Map

## Key Insights

- Life expectancy shows a positive relationship with GDP per capita, although economic performance alone does not explain every country's health outcomes.
- Internet penetration and GDP per capita show a positive association.
- Countries differ significantly in health and economic indicators, highlighting global inequalities.
- Comparing multiple indicators provides a better understanding of development than relying on GDP alone.

These findings describe associations in the dataset, not necessarily cause-and-effect relationships.
